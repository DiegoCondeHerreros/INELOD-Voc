INELOD VOC: Description back to ToC

This section describes in detail the contents of INELOD VOC and shows, by means of synthetic examples, how the vocabulary is used to build a complete RDF data cube. The exhaustive, term-by-term description of classes and properties can be found in the cross-reference section generated with LODE.

Conceptual structure

As anticipated in the overview, the ontology does not propose a new taxonomic hierarchy: it contains a flat catalogue of object properties that play one of the roles defined by the RDF Data Cube Vocabulary (Cyganiak and Reynolds, eds., 2014). Specifically, each term of the vocabulary is functionally classified as:

  • Dimension: a property whose domain is qb:Observation; it identifies the axis along which an observation is disaggregated. It subclassifies, where applicable, one of the five SDMX-RDF dimensions (refArea, refPeriod, age, currency, educationLev).
  • Measure: a property rdfs:subPropertyOf sdmx-measure:obsValue; it carries the quantitative value associated with the observation.
  • Auxiliary resource: the class ine:MeasureSet together with the property ine:inMeasureSet, which makes it possible to logically group related measures (for example, all monetary measures).

URI patterns

The INELOD project adopts the following URI patterns, aligned with the Spanish «Technical Interoperability Norms. Reuse of Information Resources» (NTI-RISP) of the National Interoperability Framework, and with the recommendations of the W3C Data on the Web Best Practices (W3C, 2017):

Resource typeURI pattern
Vocabulary term (class, property) http://lod.ine.es/def/vocabulary/{localName}
Data set (qb:DataSet) http://lod.ine.es/recurso/{operation}/DS_{name}
Data structure definition (qb:DataStructureDefinition) http://lod.ine.es/recurso/{operation}/DSD_{name}
Observation (qb:Observation) http://lod.ine.es/recurso/{operation}/observacion/{id}

Example 1: cube of births by autonomous community and year

The first example corresponds to a simple cube based on the Vital Statistics (Movimiento Natural de la Población, MNP), one of INE's continuous statistical operations (operation 30301). The cube records, for each pair autonomous community × year, the absolute number of births and the crude birth rate per thousand inhabitants. The numerical values in the example are illustrative, not official figures.

Definition of the data structure (DSD)

@prefix ine:           <http://lod.ine.es/def/vocabulary/> .
@prefix qb:            <http://purl.org/linked-data/cube#> .
@prefix sdmx-dimension:<http://purl.org/linked-data/sdmx/2009/dimension#> .
@prefix sdmx-attribute:<http://purl.org/linked-data/sdmx/2009/attribute#> .
@prefix dct:           <http://purl.org/dc/terms/> .
@prefix rdfs:          <http://www.w3.org/2000/01/rdf-schema#> .
@prefix xsd:           <http://www.w3.org/2001/XMLSchema#> .

# DSD: Births by autonomous community and reference year
<http://lod.ine.es/recurso/movimiento-natural-poblacion/DSD_NacimientosCcaaAnio>
    a qb:DataStructureDefinition ;
    rdfs:label "DSD: Nacimientos por CCAA y año"@es ,
               "DSD: Births by autonomous community and year"@en ;
    qb:component
        [ qb:dimension ine:ccaa                        ; qb:order 1 ] ,
        [ qb:dimension sdmx-dimension:refPeriod        ; qb:order 2 ] ,
        [ qb:measure   ine:births                                  ] ,
        [ qb:measure   ine:birthsPerThousandInhabitants            ] ,
        [ qb:attribute sdmx-attribute:unitMeasure                  ] .

Definition of the data set

<http://lod.ine.es/recurso/movimiento-natural-poblacion/DS_NacimientosCcaaAnio>
    a qb:DataSet ;
    rdfs:label "Nacimientos por CCAA y año"@es ,
               "Births by autonomous community and year"@en ;
    dct:title  "Births by autonomous community and year"@en ;
    dct:publisher <http://lod.ine.es/recurso/organizacion/INE> ;
    dct:issued "2025-09-23"^^xsd:date ;
    qb:structure <http://lod.ine.es/recurso/movimiento-natural-poblacion/DSD_NacimientosCcaaAnio> .

Observations

Every cell of the cube is an instance of qb:Observation that links to its qb:DataSet and provides one value for each dimension and measure declared in its DSD. Two observations are shown to illustrate the instantiation pattern: 

# Observation: Andalusia, 2023
<http://lod.ine.es/recurso/movimiento-natural-poblacion/observacion/nac-01-2023>
    a qb:Observation ;
    qb:dataSet <http://lod.ine.es/recurso/movimiento-natural-poblacion/DS_NacimientosCcaaAnio> ;
    ine:ccaa <http://datos.ine.es/recurso/territorio/ccaa/01> ;            # Andalusia
    sdmx-dimension:refPeriod <http://reference.data.gov.uk/id/year/2023> ;
    ine:births                          "55012"^^xsd:integer ;
    ine:birthsPerThousandInhabitants    "6.43"^^xsd:decimal .

# Observation: Catalonia, 2023
<http://lod.ine.es/recurso/movimiento-natural-poblacion/observacion/nac-09-2023>
    a qb:Observation ;
    qb:dataSet <http://lod.ine.es/recurso/movimiento-natural-poblacion/DS_NacimientosCcaaAnio> ;
    ine:ccaa <http://datos.ine.es/recurso/territorio/ccaa/09> ;            # Catalonia
    sdmx-dimension:refPeriod <http://reference.data.gov.uk/id/year/2023> ;
    ine:births                          "53450"^^xsd:integer ;
    ine:birthsPerThousandInhabitants    "6.78"^^xsd:decimal .

Notice that the territorial dimension ine:ccaa has range skos:Concept, so its value is the URI of a SKOS concept representing the autonomous community. Similarly, sdmx-dimension:refPeriod points to a canonical temporal resource published by the UK Government and widely reused in European Linked Data.

Example 2: cube of deaths by cause, sex and age group

The second example models a richer cube, based on the Deaths by Cause statistics (INE operation 30417). This is one of the statistical products with greatest use in epidemiology and public health, and it codes causes of death according to the International Statistical Classification of Diseases and Related Health Problems, 10th revision (ICD-10), of the WHO.

Definition of the data structure (DSD)

<http://lod.ine.es/recurso/defunciones-causa-muerte/DSD_DefuncionesCausaSexoEdadCcaa>
    a qb:DataStructureDefinition ;
    rdfs:label "DSD: Deaths by cause, sex, age group and autonomous community"@en ;
    qb:component
        [ qb:dimension ine:ccaa                ; qb:order 1 ] ,
        [ qb:dimension sdmx-dimension:refPeriod; qb:order 2 ] ,
        [ qb:dimension sdmx-dimension:sex      ; qb:order 3 ] ,
        [ qb:dimension ine:ageGroups           ; qb:order 4 ] ,
        [ qb:dimension ine:causeOfDeath        ; qb:order 5 ] ,
        [ qb:measure   ine:deaths                          ] ,
        [ qb:measure   ine:deathsPer100KInhabitants        ] .

Observation (women aged 65-69, acute myocardial infarction, Andalusia, 2023)

<http://lod.ine.es/recurso/defunciones-causa-muerte/observacion/def-01-2023-F-65-69-I21>
    a qb:Observation ;
    qb:dataSet <http://lod.ine.es/recurso/defunciones-causa-muerte/DS_DefuncionesCausaSexoEdadCcaa> ;
    ine:ccaa <http://datos.ine.es/recurso/territorio/ccaa/01> ;
    sdmx-dimension:refPeriod <http://reference.data.gov.uk/id/year/2023> ;
    sdmx-dimension:sex <http://purl.org/linked-data/sdmx/2009/code#sex-F> ;
    ine:ageGroups <http://datos.ine.es/recurso/clasificacion/grupo-edad/65-69> ;
    ine:causeOfDeath <http://id.who.int/icd/release/10/2019/I21> ;
    ine:deaths                       "234"^^xsd:integer ;
    ine:deathsPer100KInhabitants     "112.8"^^xsd:decimal .

This observation shows the power of the model: each cell unambiguously combines five dimensions (territory, period, sex, age and cause) and two measures, all of them referenced through stable, dereferenceable URIs. A data consumer can follow each URI to obtain the corresponding SKOS label, the full ICD-10 hierarchy or the set of Spanish autonomous communities, all without negotiating any further format.

SPARQL query example

The following query, run against the cube of Example 1, retrieves the crude birth rate of all autonomous communities in 2023, ordered from highest to lowest: 

PREFIX qb:             <http://purl.org/linked-data/cube#>
PREFIX ine:            <http://lod.ine.es/def/vocabulary/>
PREFIX sdmx-dimension: <http://purl.org/linked-data/sdmx/2009/dimension#>
PREFIX kos-year:       <http://reference.data.gov.uk/id/year/>

SELECT ?ccaa ?birthRate
WHERE {
  ?obs a qb:Observation ;
       qb:dataSet <http://lod.ine.es/recurso/movimiento-natural-poblacion/DS_NacimientosCcaaAnio> ;
       ine:ccaa ?ccaa ;
       sdmx-dimension:refPeriod kos-year:2023 ;
       ine:birthsPerThousandInhabitants ?birthRate .
}
ORDER BY DESC(?birthRate)

An analogous query, relying solely on generic SDMX properties (sdmx-measure:obsValue, sdmx-dimension:refArea), would still work over the same data thanks to the rdfs:subPropertyOf declarations in the vocabulary; this is precisely the interoperability benefit described in the Introduction.

Extending the vocabulary

Whenever an INE statistical operation requires a dimension or measure that is not yet covered by INELOD VOC, the recommended workflow is:

  1. Check whether the property can be modelled as rdfs:subPropertyOf of an existing term in sdmx-dimension, sdmx-measure or ine itself.
  2. Mint the new URI under the http://lod.ine.es/def/vocabulary/{localName} pattern, in lowerCamelCase, in English and semantically unambiguous.
  3. Annotate the term with rdfs:label and skos:prefLabel in Spanish and English, and with rdfs:comment or skos:definition describing operationally the content of the variable, citing the statistical operation of origin.
  4. Declare the domain (qb:Observation for dimensions) and the range (skos:Concept if the dimension is governed by a controlled list; rdfs:Resource, xsd:decimal or another type as appropriate for measures).

Extension proposals are channelled through the project's issue tracker, following the practice common to community-maintained Linked Data vocabularies (see, in the Spanish municipal domain, the Issue Management guidelines of the Ciudades Abiertas project).
