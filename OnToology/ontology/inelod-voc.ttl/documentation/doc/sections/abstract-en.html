Abstract

INELOD VOC is an OWL ontology that defines the dimension and measure properties used to represent, as Linked Data (RDF), the statistical data cubes published by the Spanish National Statistics Institute (Instituto Nacional de Estadística, INE). The vocabulary reuses the abstract model of the RDF Data Cube Vocabulary (Cyganiak and Reynolds, eds., W3C Rec. 2014) and aligns with the generic dimensions and measures of the SDMX standard (ISO 17369), so that each INE-specific property is declared, where appropriate, as rdfs:subPropertyOf of its corresponding SDMX term, ensuring downstream interoperability with SDMX-RDF queries.

Its scope is transversal to INE's entire statistical production —demography, economy and national accounts, labour market, prices, health, education, agriculture, justice, tourism and environment— and it provides a controlled, unique and dereferenceable set of URIs on which to build reusable qb:DataStructureDefinition and qb:Observation instances. This document describes the ontology, its modelling conventions and URI patterns, and illustrates its use through synthetic data cubes with their corresponding SPARQL queries.
